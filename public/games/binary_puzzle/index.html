Bitflip

The binary puzzle, also known as Takuzu or Binairo. Fill every socket with a 0 or a 1.

Rules

  1. Each cell holds a 0 or a 1.
  2. No more than two equal digits side by side or stacked: never three in a row.
  3. Every row and every column holds as many 0s as 1s.
  4. No two rows are the same, and no two columns are the same.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and logic alone gets you there.

Reading the board

  • Low, ringed discs are clues and stay put.
  • Green lamp: that row or column is full and legal.
  • Red lamp: that line breaks a rule.

Click or tap a socket to cycle 0, 1, empty; right-click or long-press steps back. Keyboard: arrow keys move, 0 or 1 places, Backspace clears.
